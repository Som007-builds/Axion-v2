## Why v1 was near-silent

- Dev rows covered by v1: **151**, missing predictions **0**, parse failures **0**.
- v1 predicted `RELIES_ON` on **12 of 151** dev rows.
- v1 recall on dev: **4/16** = 25.0% [10.2%, 49.5%]; false-positive rate **8/135** = 5.9%.
- A's text available for **122 of 151** dev rows (93 with a claim, 29 abstract-only, 29 stubs with none). Among the 16 positives, 12 had a claim and 2 only an abstract.
- v1 flagged RELIES_ON on 9/93 rows with a claim and 3/58 rows without
- Rows whose context mentions the retraction: 12.

**Diagnosis.** v1 answered a three-way classification with no confidence, so no threshold existed to tune or to escalate on, and every ambiguous row fell to MENTIONS. A's text was available for 122 of 151 dev rows (93 with a claim, 29 with an abstract only) and absent only for the 29 stubs, so missing context does not explain the silence: the framing of the question does.

## What v2 changes

- The prompt asks the guideline's counterfactual as the *only* question and returns a **0-100 probability** instead of a class, so a threshold exists to tune and a confidence exists to escalate on (prompt `p3-05-llm-v2`).
- Bands: <= 30 not dependent, 31-69 unclear, >= 70 dependent; the override rules (corroboration, citing-as-retracted, protocol use) are stated in the prompt rather than left to inference.

## Criteria

From CONTEXT §4: at least **30 retracted OA source papers with full text** and
at least **300 citation contexts citing them**. Selection follows the measured
minimums, not topic appeal. A category passes only if its **lower bound** clears
both minimums.

## Recommendation

**infectious-disease** is the proposed sub-topic: its conservative
lower bound is 653 source papers and
3,498 contexts, clearing both minimums.
Its estimated corpus of 4,749 papers fits the PRD's 2,000-5,000 target.
Among candidates that clear the minimums, the recommendation prefers a corpus
that fits the target and then the widest context margin. The ADR records the
alternatives and the estimate method; the owner makes the final choice.

## Method and limitations

- Fixed seed `20261114`; samples are uniform over eligible records.
- Source availability uses a sample of up to 300 linkable retractions per category because resolving all 68k rows live would be impolite.
- Citer OA share uses a sample of up to 200 MEDLINE citers per category; preprints are excluded from context measurement.
- Context counts come from fetched JATS only. A reference matches by DOI, PMID, or matched normalized title + year (±1); this is why reference-DOI coverage alone is not used.
- Estimated totals multiply independent point estimates; the ranges combine independent Wilson bounds and are **not** a joint confidence interval.
- Europe PMC's citations endpoint takes a PMID (`MED/<id>/citations`); a PMCID path returns 404. This was verified live and is used throughout.

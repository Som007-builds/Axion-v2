## Limitations

- Single labeler until the blind relabel lands; agreement is reported by `scripts/audit_validate.py`.
- The audit covers 200 claims from a seeded paper sample; claims within a paper are not independent, hence the paper-level cluster bootstrap.
- Silver labels are teacher output and are never used for evaluation; gold labels live only in `eval/audit/`.
- Generation config is fixed by ADR-0019 and not tuned against this audit.

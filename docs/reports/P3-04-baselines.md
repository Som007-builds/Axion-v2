# P3-04 - dependency baselines

Generated by `python scripts/dependency_baselines.py`. Every number is its output.

Positive class is `RELIES_ON`; negatives are `MENTIONS` + `UNRELATED`; `insufficient_context` rows are excluded. Weights are the design weights from the wave manifests, so a weighted figure estimates the corpus the sample came from.

## Baselines

- **citation-level** - the 'all affected' baseline: every citation counts as `RELIES_ON`. It is the floor the staged detector has to beat on false alarms.
- **section/lexical heuristic** - section prior plus a fixed lexicon from the guideline (no labels were mined for it), tuned on **dev only** over 30 grid points; chosen `section_scale=0.5`, `threshold=0.0`.
- **LLM-only** - `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, prompt `p3-04-llm-v1`, one call per row, through `LLMClient` so every call is in the ledger.

## What the test numbers mean

- Test has **21 `RELIES_ON` rows in total, 18 of them in the text-bearing frame**. Any recall estimate over that few positives has a **wide interval**: the CI column is the honest reading, not the point estimate alone.
- The heuristic was tuned on **dev only** (both frames, 151 rows). Its full grid is in the JSON summary, so the search is auditable rather than implied.
- Test numbers were computed once and frozen in `eval/audit/p3-04/p3-04-test-frozen.json` (spec `21faf8c921c32fb8`). A later run recomputes them and refuses to continue if any frozen number moved. A baseline added after the freeze (the LLM one, which needs live calls) is recorded with its own `added_at` stamp instead of silently replacing the frozen file.
- The stub stratum is reported separately and never pooled into the primary frame: those rows have no source claim, so no text-based method can use A's claim there.

## Reading

- **citation-level** is the floor: recall 100% by construction, precision equal to the base rate (16.4% on the text-bearing test), and a false-alarm rate of 100% - it flags every citation.
- **heuristic** trades recall for false alarms: on the text-bearing test recall 83.3% [60.8%, 94.2%], precision 30.0%, false-alarm rate 38.0% [28.8%, 48.2%] (dev: 85.7% recall, 42.6% false alarms). It is bounded by what a section and a word list can see: it cannot tell whether B's argument needs A.
- **LLM-only** is far more specific and far less sensitive: precision 75.0%, recall 16.7% [5.8%, 39.2%], false-alarm rate 1.1% on the text-bearing test - it finds few positives, so alone it answers the wrong question. Its value is as the escalation tier in P3-05, not as a standalone detector.
- With 18 text-bearing test positives (21 overall), the difference between the heuristic's and the LLM's recall is larger than either interval is precise about; neither a claim of superiority nor of equivalence is supported yet.

- Every number here is conditional on the P1 citer set (ADR-0015), on one labeler for the positive class, and on the design weights of two waves with different base rates.

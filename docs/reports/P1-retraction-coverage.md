## Method and limitations

- Snapshot counts are exact for the CSV at the recorded commit.
- Europe PMC rates are sample estimates: only the sampled records were queried, so the CIs describe sampling uncertainty, not Europe PMC indexing error.
- A record missing here may still have full text elsewhere; this measures Europe PMC availability, which is what the corpus can ingest.
- Retraction Watch lists the *original paper* identifiers in the fields used (`OriginalPaperDOI` / `OriginalPaperPubMedID`); retraction notices are separate.
